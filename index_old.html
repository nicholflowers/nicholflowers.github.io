About

In tech since 2001. I added a rigorous foundation in machine learning and statistics to a career already spent shipping real-world systems.

I build machine learning models that work in the real world, not just in development.

I've spent more than twenty years in tech as [your role / field, e.g., a software engineer in enterprise SaaS]. I recently earned [your master's in data science], not to start over but to bring formal depth in machine learning, statistics, and modeling to the kinds of problems I already know how to deliver. Two decades of building things that have to actually work shapes how I approach every model: I care less about mathematically elegant answers and more about whether the thing holds up in production.

That training culminated in Eye2Voice, a gaze-estimation model powering an assistive communication tool, which earned the UC Berkeley Hal R. Varian Award for Top Capstone Project. The technical work was a deep dive into computer vision, class imbalance, and model evaluation, but the part that stuck with me was the gap between what a confusion matrix says and what a user actually experiences. I build for that gap.

"The model is the easy part. Making it hold up in the real world is the job."

Across the rest of my portfolio, I work in classification, prediction, NLP, research design, and applied analysis, spanning healthcare, finance, public safety, and civic data.
